JevTrends is a place to discover projects, then follow the people who made them. Each entry links to its original post or repository so you can inspect the context behind a short summary.

## Summaries are starting points

Our descriptions explain the idea in our own words. They are not endorsements, implementation reviews, or promises that a demo will behave the same way in your application. A repository can also evolve after an entry is added.

## Unknown is useful information

When a source does not report cost or latency, we leave that measurement unavailable. When it does, we identify it as the author's report. That keeps an isolated demo result from becoming a general performance claim.

## Different numbers answer different questions

Likes and replies describe activity on X. Pageviews describe traffic to a website. Neither proves that a project is reliable, and one cannot stand in for the other. Our directory remains usable when those numbers are missing.
